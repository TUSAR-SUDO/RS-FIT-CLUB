# Photography credits

Every photograph on this site is licensed stock from [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/license/):
free to use for commercial purposes, no attribution required. They are credited here anyway,
and the footer says so in small print. **None of these photos are of RS Fit Club or its
members.** They are stand-ins: real photographs of this floor, sent from a phone, replace them
one for one — drop them into `FILES/photos/` under the same file name and run `npm run photos`.

The `credit` line below is the field each image carries in `src/data/photos.ts`, written there
by `scripts/build_photos.py`.
